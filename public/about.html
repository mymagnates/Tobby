About Handout

Built from the operational reality of managing property.

Handout is created by Magnates Solution LLC in Plano, Texas for owners and property managers who need a cleaner way to keep records, tasks, money movement, leases, and reminders connected to the property they belong to.

home_work One property workspace

A practical system for day-to-day execution, long-term visibility, and shared access.

history

Why we started

After more than 12 years of hands-on home ownership and property management work, we saw the same problems repeat: scattered documents, manual follow-ups, delayed communication, and too many disconnected tools.

fact_check

What we focus on

Handout is designed to organize property data, standardize recurring work, support better owner-manager communication, and keep decisions tied to reliable records.

hub

How we build

We prioritize features that reduce friction: documents, maintenance, transactions, leases, reminders, shared access, and service coordination around each property.

Operating principles

Simple enough to use, structured enough to trust.
